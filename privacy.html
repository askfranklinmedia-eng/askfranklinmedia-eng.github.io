Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Franklin Media ("we", "us") makes promotional images and videos for businesses. This policy explains how our app "Franklin Media Drive" uses Google account data.

What the app does

Franklin Media Drive is an internal tool used only by Franklin Media. It uploads finished image and video files that we have made for our clients to our own Google Drive, and creates share links so clients can download full-quality copies.

Google data we access

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. This lets it see and manage only the files that the app itself has uploaded. It cannot see, read or change any other files in a Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, contacts, calendars or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • Files are uploaded only to deliver work to our clients.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Access tokens are stored securely on our own server and are used only for uploads and share links.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Client files and data

Files shared with clients stay on our Google Drive and may be removed after delivery. Clients can ask us to delete any file at any time.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Access granted to the app can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
