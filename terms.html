Terms & Refunds

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These terms explain how working with Ask Franklin Media ("we", "us") works, how payment works and when you can get a refund. They apply to every business we make videos or images for ("you").

In short: your first set of promo images is free. After that, videos and further images have no upfront cost. You see the finished work first, then pay what you think it's worth, or nothing if it isn't right for you. If something goes wrong with a payment, we'll put it right.

1. Who we are

Ask Franklin Media is a sole trader based in South Yorkshire, UK, making promotional videos and images for independent businesses. You can contact us at askfranklinmedia@gmail.com.

2. What we provide

  • Promotional videos (vertical shorts for social media and widescreen videos for websites) and promotional images, made around your products, venue and branding.
  • Everything is delivered digitally, by email or as a download link (for example Google Drive). Nothing physical is sent.
  • Before you pay, we're happy to make reasonable changes to the finished work, such as text, timing or music.

3. How pricing works

  • Free first promo images. The first set of promo images we make for you is free, with no obligation.
  • No upfront cost for videos or further images. Any videos, and any images after that first free set, are made first, with no deposit, contract or subscription. You see the finished work before paying.
  • You name the price. If you're happy with the work, you pay what you think it's worth through a secure Stripe payment link or invoice. If it isn't right for you, you're not obliged to pay anything.
  • If we agree a fixed price with you in writing for a particular piece of work, that agreed price applies instead.
  • All prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.

4. Payment

  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Pay by Bank.
  • We never see or store your full card details.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days unless we agree otherwise.

5. Refunds

Because you only pay after seeing the finished work, and you choose the amount, a payment you make is normally final. We will always refund you in these cases:

  • Payment mistakes. If you're charged twice or pay the wrong amount by mistake, we'll refund the duplicate or the difference in full.
  • Faulty files. If a file you paid for won't open or play, or isn't the version we showed you, we'll send a working copy. If we can't fix it, we'll refund you in full.
  • Changed your mind. If you've paid and feel it wasn't worth it, tell us within 14 days of paying. We'll either put it right or refund some or all of your payment, and if we refund in full, please stop using the work.

To ask for a refund, email askfranklinmedia@gmail.com with your business name and the date of payment. We'll reply within 2 working days. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually arrive within 5 to 10 working days.

6. Cancelling

You can stop at any time before paying, with nothing to pay and no reason needed. There are no subscriptions or recurring charges unless you ask for one and agree to it in writing.

  • We will not show your images or videos on our own website, social media or anywhere else unless you give us permission first. If you do give permission, you can withdraw it at any time and we'll take them down.

8. Your materials

When you send us logos, photos or other material, you confirm you have the right to let us use them. We only use them to make your work, and we'll delete them on request. See our privacy policy for how we handle data.

We aim to make work that brings your business more attention, but we can't guarantee particular results, such as views, sales or customers. Our total responsibility to you for any piece of work is limited to the amount you paid for it. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have by law.
